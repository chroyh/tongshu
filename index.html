QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked

Is my birth data really private?
Yes. The BaZi calculator runs entirely in your browser — your birth date, time, and place never leave your device. We have no server collecting this data. You can verify by opening browser DevTools and watching the Network tab while you calculate.
How is this different from Cafe Astrology or Astro.com?
Three differences: (1) We integrate Eastern BaZi with Western astrology in one report — most apps offer one tradition. (2) Every interpretive conclusion cites its classical source. (3) The free tier is genuinely useful — no paywall on the basics.
Will BaZi tell me when I'll die or marry?
No, and we refuse to model those questions even when asked. BaZi describes personality, temperament, and cyclical patterns — not deterministic predictions. See our CE-06 essay on what we won't predict.
What are the 9,709 classical quotes?
Excerpts from five canonical Chinese metaphysics texts: Dripping Heaven Jade (滴天髓), Three Lives Compendium (三命通会), Zi Ping True Treatise (子平真诠), Exhaustion-Penetration Precious Mirror (穷通宝鉴), and Deep-Sea Zi Ping (渊海子平). Each interpretation we generate cites which text and section it came from.
Do you offer refunds on the Full Reading?
Yes — full refund within 14 days if the report wasn't generated or if the technical delivery failed. Since these are digital reports tied to your specific birth data, we can't refund once the report has been delivered successfully.
Why the name "Celestial Atelier"?
An atelier is a craftsman's workshop — a place where skilled hands shape raw material into art. We see your birth chart as raw material, and the cosmic systems as the craftsman's tools. Our atelier is where they meet.
